Robot build

The chassis is a BaseBot-style frame with front-wheel drive. The tyre wheels are on the two motors at the front, and free omni wheels are at the rear. Four L corner connectors hold the brain, and its screen faces the person behind the robot. Each sensor has a fixed mount: switch it on or off, and choose its Smart Port.
